Contact

Praxa is made by one person. Email is the best way to reach us — support, bugs, questions about your data, or just telling us the app got something wrong.

For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — plus your iPhone model and iOS version.

From inside the app

The Fast feedback card in Settings sends a note without leaving Praxa, and attaches your app version so we don't have to ask. Your email address is optional there — but we can only reply if you include it.

Your data

Questions about what Praxa stores, or a request to delete feedback you've sent, go to the same address. What we hold and how long we hold it is written out in the Privacy Policy.
