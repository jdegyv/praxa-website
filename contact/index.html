Contact

The best way to reach me is to send me an email (praxa.app@gmail.com).

If you're reporting a bug, it helps if you say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happens instead — plus your iPhone model and iOS version.

Feedback is always appreciated. In addition to what changes you'd like to see, it helps if you give context as to why: what would that change allow you to do, or what would it stop?

You can also send feedback from inside the app's Settings page.
